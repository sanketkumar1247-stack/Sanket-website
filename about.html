Biography

Researcher, writer, and scholar of global order.

I am a PhD Research Scholar at the Centre for European Studies, School of International Studies, Jawaharlal Nehru University, New Delhi. My research examines the European Union’s regulatory power, digital governance, and the evolving political economy of India–Europe cooperation.

My work brings together international relations, political economy, and policy analysis to ask how digital rules travel across borders, shape global norms, and affect the choices of states. I also write for public audiences on diplomacy, technology, trade, and India–EU relations.
